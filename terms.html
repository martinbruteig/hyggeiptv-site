Hygge Premium

Everything in the app is free for 3 days from the first time you play something from your own playlist. After that, playing needs Hygge Premium: a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Premium is the player only and includes no channels, films or series.

Payment is charged to your Apple account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. You manage and cancel them in Settings on your device, under your name, Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple.

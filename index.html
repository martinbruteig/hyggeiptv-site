Hygge IPTV is a fast, reliable and easy-to-use player for iPhone and iPad. Add the playlist you already have, find what you want in seconds, and send it to your TV with Chromecast or AirPlay.

From screenshot to watching

No typing of long addresses. Take a picture of the details from your provider, or pick a screenshot, and Hygge fills in the rest.

  1. Scan the details with the camera, or choose a screenshot.
  2. Check what Hygge found: server, username and password (Xtream Codes API login), or an M3U link.
  3. Watch. Your channels open the next time in a moment, straight from the device.

Chromecast. It just works.

Pick your TV, tap a channel, and it plays. No black screen, no extra setup. AirPlay is there too.

  • Chromecast, Android TV and Nvidia Shield
  • AirPlay to Apple TV and AirPlay TVs
  • Change channel from the phone while the TV plays

Thousands of channels, still tidy

Channels are gathered by country and by theme, the small groups are kept together, and there is a search field in every folder. Rows show what is on now when your playlist has a guide.

  • Favourites and recently watched first
  • Search across everything, or inside one country
  • Films and episodes continue where you stopped
  • Several playlists, each with its own favourites

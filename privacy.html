Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Hygge IPTV collects nothing about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and sends no information to us, Aksjefokus AS.

What stays on your device

Your playlists, programme guide, favourites and viewing history are stored on the device only. Logins and playlist links are kept in the device keychain. All of it is deleted when you remove the playlist or the app.

Who the app contacts

  • Your provider, at the addresses in the playlist you added, to fetch channels, guide, pictures and streams. Your provider sees these requests, including your IP address.
  • Your TV, on your local network, when you use AirPlay or Chromecast. Chromecast uses Google's Cast software, under Google's privacy policy.

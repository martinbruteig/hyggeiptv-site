Support

Write to support@hyggeiptv.app. Say what you did, what happened, and which iPhone or iPad you use. Never send your password.

We cannot help with channels or subscriptions. Hygge IPTV is only a player. For missing channels or an ended subscription, ask your provider.

A channel does not play

Choose Try again, or pick another quality if the channel has one. If nothing plays, open the playlist menu and choose Update now.

Sending to a TV

The phone and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network. For Chromecast, allow access to the local network when iOS asks, and keep the app open while casting.

Removing your data

Open the playlist menu and choose Remove this playlist, or delete the app.
